Example networks

The 13 networks distributed with the R package netmeta, converted to contrast level.

Diabetes: glucose lowering agents Mean difference in HbA1c Ten oral treatments for type 2 diabetes on the change in HbA1c.
COPD: mortality on inhaled treatments Odds ratio for mortality Three trials, one of them four-armed. Small enough to follow every path by eye.
Depression in primary care Odds ratio for early response Ninety-three trials of twenty-two treatments, the largest network here.
Atrial fibrillation: stroke prevention Odds ratio for stroke Several trials have a zero event count, so the continuity correction is visible.
Smoking cessation counselling Odds ratio for cessation Four counselling strategies and several three-arm trials.
Textbook network of four treatments Effect, arbitrary units Every effect 1, every standard error 1, so the arithmetic can be checked by hand.
